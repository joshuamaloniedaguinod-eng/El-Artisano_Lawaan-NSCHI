El Artisano

Tapat at Responsableng Pamamahayag.

El Artisano is the online school publication of Lawaan National School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries. It is a space for student journalism, feature writing, campus updates, creative expression, and responsible visual commentary.

Our guiding values are accuracy, fairness, respect, creativity, and service to the school community. All stories shown in this starter website are sample placeholder content and should be reviewed by the publication adviser and editorial team before publishing.
